Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is RiseReps?
RiseReps is a workout alarm clock for iPhone. Instead of a snooze button, the alarm only turns off after your camera verifies that you completed a set of push-ups, squats, or jumping jacks. It's built for heavy sleepers, chronic snoozers, and anyone trying to wake up early and build a morning exercise habit.
How does the camera count my reps?
RiseReps uses on-device pose detection to track your body and count each rep in real time. Everything runs locally on your iPhone — no video or images ever leave your device, and nothing is uploaded or recorded.
Can I snooze or just tap to dismiss the alarm?
No. There's no snooze button and no tap-to-dismiss. The alarm keeps going until your reps are verified. Strictness modes go further: Persistent mode re-fires the alarm every 60 seconds if you close the app, and Full Lockdown blocks your other apps until the workout is complete.
Which exercises can turn off the alarm?
Push-ups, squats, or jumping jacks. You pick the rep count, and built-in progression can automatically increase your reps each week so the habit keeps growing with you.
Is RiseReps good for heavy sleepers?
Yes — that's exactly who it's built for. Physical movement is the most reliable way to fully wake up. Because dismissing the alarm requires real, camera-verified exercise, you can't half-wake, tap something, and fall back asleep.
How is RiseReps different from Alarmy, Wayk, or math-problem alarms?
Mission alarms use puzzles, math problems, QR codes, or photo missions — tasks you can do half-asleep from bed. RiseReps requires verified physical movement: the camera watches you complete real reps with on-device pose detection, so your body is actually up and moving before the alarm stops.
Is RiseReps free?
RiseReps is free to download on the App Store and includes a free trial on the annual plan, with weekly and annual options after that. The annual plan works out to well under a dollar a week.
